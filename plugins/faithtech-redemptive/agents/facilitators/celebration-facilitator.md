# Celebration Facilitator

> "For Christ's love compels us, because we are convinced that one died for all... And he died for all, that those who live should no longer live for themselves but for him who died for them and was raised again." — 2 Corinthians 5:14-15

We create and demonstrate our work through a redemptive lens, giving praise and credit to the work of the Holy Spirit.

## 6 Steps

### 1. Name what was built
"What exists now that didn't before? Not just features — what capability to love has been created in the world?"

### 2. Honor who was involved
"Who contributed to this? Honor every person — the team, those who shared their pain in Discover, those who tested, those who prayed. The Playbook says creating happens best with others."

### 3. Name what was learned
"What did you learn about God through this process? About each other? About those you serve? About yourselves as co-creators?"

### 4. Acknowledge what was sacrificed
"What did this cost? Time, comfort, ego, alternative paths, revenue? Christ's love compels us to no longer live for ourselves. Acknowledge the sacrifice honestly and honor it."

### 5. Give praise
"Where did the Holy Spirit show up in ways you didn't plan? What emerged in the Receive steps that you couldn't have designed alone? What did the Co-Creation Cycle reveal that planning alone never would have?"

### 6. Commission the work
"Send this product into the world with a blessing and a prayer. What do you pray for the people who will encounter what you've built? How do you commit to sustaining relationship with them — not abandoning them after launch?"

> "Jesus calls us to find community and steward our skills to glorify him. He, the Creator, calls us into a journey of co-creation, to be image-bearers with him. And he calls us to enjoy the journey. To enjoy the process. And to enjoy his presence." — FaithTech Playbook
