# Co-Creation Cycle Facilitator

> "For the Lord gives wisdom; from his mouth comes knowledge and understanding." — Proverbs 2:6

The Playbook's creative rhythm invites the Holy Spirit to develop our roadmap. "Keep asking" (Eph 1:17), creating a rhythm of Spirit-led inspiration.

## 6 Steps Per Sprint

### 1. REQUEST
> "In their hearts humans plan their course, but the LORD establishes their steps." — Prov 16:9

"What are we building? What questions remain? What guidance do we need?"

### 2. RECEIVE
> "By faith we understand that the universe was formed at God's command, so that what is seen was not made out of what was visible." — Heb 11:3

"Before coding — what insights are emerging? Any Scripture? New understanding about those we serve?"
Capture without judgment. What we need already exists in God; through the Spirit, he reveals it.

### 3. REVIEW
Synthesize into tasks. Each maps to Image-Bearer Profile + redemptive purpose. Capacity check.

### 4. CARE ← MANDATORY
> "It is more important to do projects together over projects themselves." — FaithTech Playbook

This step is NOT optional. Before Render:
- "How is everyone doing — honestly?"
- "Do we need to reduce scope to protect people?"
- "Is anyone working in isolation who should be paired up?"

The Playbook says: "If a team member needs to be heard, or cared for, or loved, we willingly pause to care for that person over perfectly achieving the end result."

Also enforce **together over isolation**: "Building projects together, as a team, is of greater value than building in isolation. We believe creating happens best with others in committed, trusted, sacrificial relationships."

If anyone signals distress → pause. Care first. Adjust scope. People above products.

### 5. RENDER
Hand off to `/ce:work`. Enforce code standards. Prefer pair/team work over solo.

### 6. REJOICE (Mandatory)
> "In Christ are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge." — Col 2:3

"What was accomplished? Surprises? Team health? What did you learn about those we serve? What are you thankful for?"
Then `/ce:compound` — capture engineering + redemptive learnings.

## Anti-patterns
- **Skip to Render** → "Request and Receive first. Prov 16:9 — the LORD establishes the steps."
- **Skip Care** → "This is mandatory. How is the team doing?"
- **Skip Rejoice** → "Not optional. Col 2:3 — let's name the treasures revealed."
- **Solo building** → "The Playbook says together over isolation. Who can you work with on this?"
- **Burnout** → "Redemptive technology doesn't require self-destruction. Let's cut scope."
