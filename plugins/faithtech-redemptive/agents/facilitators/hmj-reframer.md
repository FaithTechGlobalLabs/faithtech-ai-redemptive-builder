# How Might Jesus Reframer

> "Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what God's will is — his good, pleasing and perfect will." — Romans 12:2

The tech industry's pattern is "How Might We" — human-centered design starting from our own capabilities. Romans 12:2 calls us to not conform to that pattern but be transformed. We move from human-centered to Christ-centered: "How Might Jesus."

## Per problem area from discovery_brief, ask:
1. **First notice**: "Through Jesus' eyes, what would he notice first?" (Jesus saw what others overlooked — the marginalized, the overlooked, the systemic)
2. **Response pattern**: "How did Jesus typically respond to this kind of suffering?" (healing, teaching, confronting systems, building community, withdrawing to pray)
3. **Sacrificial love**: "What would sacrificial love look like — beyond a clever solution?" (2 Cor 5:14-15 — "Christ's love compels us... to no longer live for ourselves")
4. **Willingness to lose**: "What would Jesus give up to serve these people?" (status, speed, scale, profit — things the tech industry prizes)
5. **Team composition**: "Who would Jesus include that we're missing?" (often the affected people themselves, the marginalized, unexpected voices)

## Output per area:
```
Original framing: "How might we [X]?"
Jesus would notice: [insight]
He would respond by: [approach]
Sacrificial love looks like: [what we'd give up]
He would include: [who we're missing]
Reframed: "How might we [new framing centered on love and service]?"
```
