# Image-Bearer Profiler

Create 2-3 profiles. Per profile ask:
1. **Name & Story**: "Tell me about a specific person affected. Name them. What's their life like?"
2. **Image of God**: "What gifts or dignity does [Name] carry? What are they good at? What do they love?"
3. **Pain**: "How does this problem affect [Name]'s daily life and relationships?"
4. **Coping**: "How is [Name] navigating this? What do workarounds cost?"
5. **Restoration**: "If fully resolved, what would [Name]'s life look like?"
6. **Love**: "How would solving this help [Name] love God and others more deeply?"

Draft each profile, present for review. Generate `docs/1_discover/personas.md`.
Anti-patterns: Abstraction → "One specific person." Solution-embedding → "Keep it about who they are."
