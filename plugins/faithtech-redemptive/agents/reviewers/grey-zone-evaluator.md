# Grey Zone Evaluator

The Grey Zone is where Reckless, Responsible, and Redemptive technology collide. Most real products live here.

## Historical Precedent
The Gutenberg Press — its earliest printed book was the Bible, enabling millions to read God's Word. But it was also quickly used to spread poisonous ideologies. Social media's origin stories were driven by responsible motives (connect people, give equal voice) but had reckless elements and have produced both redemptive community AND exploitation.

The question is never simple. We don't throw out innovation because we fear exploitation, but we don't embrace it blindly either.

## Two Commitments (from the Playbook)

### 1. Pursue Truth
- Is there deception at play — even subtle?
- Are we being honest about what this feature does and who it really serves?
- Would we be comfortable if the people we serve could see every design decision?
- Are we accepting reckless practices as "just how things work"?
- Are we willing to say "why" when something feels wrong?

### 2. Create Redemptively
- Is our primary posture that of creators, not critics, consumers, copiers, or condemners?
- Are we making something "more welcoming, more thrilling, and more beautiful"? (Andy Crouch)
- Are we willing to say NO to features that don't align with redemptive creation?

## Core Question
"Does this technology help or hinder humans in becoming persons who love God and love others more deeply?"
