# Phase 0: Prepare — "Equip and Align the Community"

> "Running Create in your community can be a powerful way to address technological challenges through a redemptive lens." — FaithTech Workbook

Before entering the 4D Cycle, community leaders and team leads prepare their project, team, and timeline following the FaithTech Workbook's Prepare framework.

## Step 0.1 — Sourcing Projects and Challenges

Ask the user:
1. "How was this project or challenge identified?"
   - Options: Local ideation session, local community/neighborhood need, church or non-profit partner request, school/university engagement, or analysis of existing tech.
2. "Who are the primary partners or local communities connected to this challenge?"

Generate `docs/0_prepare/project_charter.md` — capturing the challenge origin, key partners, and initial problem context.

## Step 0.2 — Team Formation & Roles

Ask the user:
1. "Who is on the team, and what mix of technical and non-technical skills do you have?"
2. "How are team roles distributed (e.g., facilitator, lead builder, domain advisor, prayer partner)?"
3. "How will the team intentionally integrate faith and mutual care into your workflow?"

Generate `docs/0_prepare/team_formation.md` — mapping team composition, skill balance, role clarity, and spiritual care practices.

## Step 0.3 — Setting Timelines & Create Rhythms

Guide timeline selection based on FaithTech Workbook guidelines:
- **Short-term sprint** (1-4 weeks) — rapid prototyping or focused challenge
- **Medium-term project / Create12** (12 weeks / 1-3 months) — recommended standard rhythm for FaithTech community Create cycles
- **Long-term initiative** (3+ months) — multi-cycle project broken into repeating 4D cycles

Ask:
1. "Which timeline option fits your context best?"
2. "What is your meeting rhythm (e.g., weekly community meetups, bi-weekly team sprints)?"
3. "What are your key milestone target dates for Discover, Discern, Develop, and Demonstrate?"

Generate `docs/0_prepare/timeline_roadmap.md` — establishing the Create roadmap, milestone targets, and expectation management.

## Step 0.4 — Leader & Community Resources

Provide reference links from the FaithTech Workbook:
- **Discover Facilitation**: Problem identification & lament guide
- **Discern Facilitation**: "How Might Jesus" & 3RC guide
- **Develop Facilitation**: Co-Creation Cycle & sprint review
- **Demonstrate Facilitation**: Impact measurement & story sharing
- **Case Studies**: SHAREBIBLES app, Ending Human Trafficking, Aviation Charity App
