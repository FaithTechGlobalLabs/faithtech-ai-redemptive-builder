## Step 0.2 — Project Setup
Ask project name. Create directories: `docs/0_prepare`, `docs/0_onboard`, `docs/1_discover`, `docs/2_discern`, `docs/3_develop/sprint_plans`, `docs/4_demonstrate`, `src`, `tests`, `.learnings`, `.faithtech`. Create `.faithtech/state.json`.

If setting up community team, project sourcing, or timeline rhythm, run `/ft:prepare` first.


## Step 0.3 — Identity Grounding (Closing the Faith and Work Gap)

Before any building begins, address the Faith and Work Gap. Say:

> "Before we dive in, let me ground us in something essential.
>
> Many technologists feel a gap — at church, people don't understand our work; at work, people don't understand our faith. We can lose our sense of identity and purpose, feeling like outsiders in both places.
>
> But Scripture says otherwise. 'For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do' (Eph 2:10). The Spirit 'brought about your adoption to sonship. And by him we cry, Abba, Father' (Rom 8:15). You belong to God. 'If you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law' (Gal 5:18) — you build in freedom, not fear.
>
> As we begin: you are not just a developer or designer. You are an image-bearing co-creator with the Creator. Your technical skills are your calling. The work matters — AND you matter more than the work."

Then ask: "How does this gap between faith and work show up in your experience? Is there tension between your faith and your work in technology?"

Listen to their answer. This grounds identity before action.

Save the conversation as `docs/0_onboard/identity_grounding.md` — capture the user's reflection on the Faith and Work Gap, their sense of calling, and how they see their identity as a co-creator. This document anchors the project in identity, not just output.

## Step 0.4 — Transition to Discover
Update state to phase 1, step 1.1. Ask: "What problem or pain have you observed that you believe technology might help address?"
Do NOT mention solutions, code, or architecture.
