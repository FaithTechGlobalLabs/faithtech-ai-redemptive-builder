# Phase 1: Discover — "See Through the Lens of Christ"

> "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me." — John 10:27

We reorient ourselves to see, through the lens of Christ, those most affected by the problem. We position ourselves humbly through a practice of lament.

## Tool Requirements
- **CE plugin** (`ce:brainstorm`): Used in Step 1.1 for structured Q&A. If not installed, conduct the interview directly.
- **Web search**: Not required in this phase (listening is human conversation). Available if user references something to look up.

## CE Integration
Run `/ce:brainstorm` at Step 1.1 for structured Q&A, replacing CE's product-focused questions with the redemptive listening questions below.

## Optional: Theology Grounding
Before listening begins, offer to read `skills/theology-of-technology/SKILL.md` — 8 biblical narratives showing God's history with technology from Genesis to Revelation. Ask: "Would you like to reflect on how God has used technology throughout Scripture before we begin? It takes 5 minutes and grounds our work in his larger story."

## Step 1.1 — Listen: Surface the Pain
Ask one at a time. Never more than 2 per turn.

Round 1:
- "What problem or pain have you observed that you believe technology might help address?"
- "Who are the specific people most affected? Describe one as a person, not a demographic."
- "How long has this existed? What makes it urgent now?"

Round 2:
- "What does a typical day look like for someone experiencing this?"
- "What emotional toll does this take — real human cost, not just inconvenience?"
- "Have you personally witnessed this pain firsthand?"

**Anti-pattern:** Solution jumping → "Let's stay with the problem. Tell me more about the people affected."

## Step 1.2 — Listen: Understand Landscape
- "What existing solutions have these people tried? Where do they fall short?"
- "What systemic or structural causes exist beneath the symptoms?"
- "Who benefits from this problem remaining unsolved?"

**Anti-pattern:** Abstraction ("users", "the market") → "Can you bring this back to a specific person?"

## Step 1.3 — Listen: Redemptive Lens
- "How does this problem prevent people from loving God or loving others more deeply?"
- "If fully resolved — truly redeemed — what would restoration look like?"
- "Is there a biblical story or principle that resonates with this problem?"

Generate `docs/1_discover/listening_notes.md`.

## Step 1.4 — Lament

> "Lord, the God of heaven, the great and awesome God, who keeps his covenant of love with those who love him and keep his commandments, let your ear be attentive and your eyes open to hear the prayer your servant is praying before you day and night." — Nehemiah 1:5-6

Read `agents/facilitators/lament-facilitator.md`. Nehemiah modeled this when he heard about Jerusalem's destroyed walls — he wept, mourned, fasted, and prayed before he planned. We follow that model.

Guide the 6-step lament:
1. Address God  2. Describe the problem to him  3. Recall testimony  4. Petition  5. Ask for intervention  6. Praise in advance

Generate `docs/1_discover/lament.md`.

## Step 1.5 — Image-Bearer Profiles

> "Let us make mankind in our image." — Genesis 1:26

Read `agents/facilitators/image-bearer-profiler.md`. Create 2-3 profiles: Name/Story, Image of God in them, The Pain, Current Coping, Restoration Vision, Love Connection.

Generate `docs/1_discover/personas.md`.

## Step 1.6 — Synthesis
Combine listening notes + lament + personas into `docs/1_discover/discovery_brief.md`. Present for review.

## Gate Check → Phase 2
All must be affirmative:
1. "Is the problem deeply understood from affected people's perspective?"
2. "Have we moved from empathy (understanding pain) to compassion (willing to sacrifice)?"
3. "Did we start with God (lament), not solutions?"
4. "Do profiles represent real humans with inherent worth, not abstract demographics?"
